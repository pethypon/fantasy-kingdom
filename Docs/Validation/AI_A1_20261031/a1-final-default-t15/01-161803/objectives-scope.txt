Read-only AIDecisionObserver aggregation by faction and own round. Limit=4096; records=227; ignored events after record limit=0; observer errors=0.
Generated/governor/selection counts are candidate samples across existing decision events, not unique legal opportunities. Counts from different stages must not be summed as distinct actions.
In the R2 revised producer, 'generated' is emitted before the first governor filter and 'governor' immediately after it. These are generated proposals, not a counterfactual proof of every possible legal action. Older baseline telemetry was post-filter and cannot supply the same rejected-candidate census.
An observed target means a legally visible principal commander or living main crystal; memory means an existing board observation snapshot. Hidden live coordinates/HP are never inspected.
Chosen counts are selections, not successful attacks or damage. The R2 revised selector emits for empty and mistake branches too. If a producer has no selection event, selection_not_observed remains unknown rather than a rejection.
Emergency/recovery event counts record observed modes. A mixed-mode turn does not prove that its earlier crisis caused a later missed attack.
A round with no target in one decision is not classified as unseen for the whole round if any later decision observed it. Non-emitting turns are absent rather than fabricated as zero.
No paths, counterfactual ranges, reserves, or candidate sets are generated. Route/range/AP explanations remain Unknown unless represented by the existing decision evidence.
